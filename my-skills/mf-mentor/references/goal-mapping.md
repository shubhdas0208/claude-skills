# Goal → Portfolio Mapping (Handbook Ch. 10)

Order of operations — never reversed: **Goal → Horizon → Risk capacity → Asset class → Category → Fund.** Fund selection is the last and least important step.

## Horizon → category table (§10.2)

| Goal & horizon | Risk capacity | Asset class | Categories that fit | Common mistakes (never suggest) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency fund | None | Debt | Liquid, overnight, money market | Any equity; credit risk; long duration |
| < 1 yr | None | Debt | Liquid, ultra-short, money market; arbitrage if high bracket | Equity |
| 1–3 yrs | Low | Debt ± sliver of equity | Short duration, corporate bond, banking & PSU; conservative hybrid at margin | Small/mid-cap; gilt; credit risk |
| 3–5 yrs | Moderate | Mixed | Aggressive hybrid, BAF, multi-asset; large-cap/index slice | 100% small-cap; sectoral |
| 5–10 yrs | High | Mostly equity | Index/large-cap core + flexi + some mid-cap | Sector bets as core; credit-risk debt sleeve |
| 10+ yrs | Highest | Equity-dominant | Index core, flexi, mid/small satellite, international slice | Anything user won't hold through a −40% drawdown |

Note: user's emergency fund + insurance are already handled (profile) — do not re-litigate.

## Capacity vs tolerance
Capacity = arithmetic (horizon, income stability, dependents, cushion). Tolerance = temperament (the §4.5 drawdown question: "fund fell 50%+, took 3 years to recover — would your SIP have survived?"). **The LOWER of the two governs the allocation.** Record both + the governing one in the goal file.

## Core–satellite (§10.3)
- **Core 60–80% of equity money:** index fund(s) and/or 1 large-cap or flexi-cap. Boring, low-cost, zero drama.
- **Satellite 20–40%:** argued-for bets (mid/small-cap, value/contra diversifier, international). Every satellite requires a written thesis — "it had great returns" is not a thesis.
- **3–6 funds total.** Fund #7 requires firing one first.
- **Diversify across return engines, not AMC logos:** one flexi + one mid-cap + one international diversifies; two flexi-caps overlap. Overlap bar < 33% (Advisorkhoj tool, free).
- **Debt sleeve:** duration matched to need date; high-credit categories by default (≥90% sovereign+AAA).
- **Rebalance** annually or at ±5–10% drift bands — user reports current weights from Groww; app-side we only prompt and reason.
- **Glide-out:** shift equity → debt starting 2–3 years before the goal date.

## Default stance (§2.6, §11 Step 5)
A plain Nifty 50/Sensex index fund is the strongest default core. Any active fund must answer: "what makes this an exception to the base rate, and does the reason survive the manager leaving?" No answer → the index fund IS the answer, recorded as a successful research outcome.
