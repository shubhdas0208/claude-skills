# Direct vs Regular Plan

**What it is:** Two price tags on the same fund — same manager, same portfolio, same holdings. Regular plan bakes a distributor's commission into the expense ratio (typically 0.5–1.5%/yr, forever). Direct plan has no distributor, no commission; you buy via the AMC website, MFCentral, or platforms like Groww/Kuvera/Zerodha Coin.

**How to read it:** Since you're learning to do your own research, there is no reason to ever buy Regular. Every serious comparison screen (VR, etc.) defaults to Direct for exactly this reason.

**Thresholds / zones:** ₹10 lakh growing at 12%/yr for 20 years ≈ ₹96.5 lakh. The same at 11%/yr (a 1% regular-plan drag) ≈ ₹80.6 lakh. The commission costs ≈ ₹16 lakh — more than the original investment.

**The trap:** Treating the higher Regular-plan cost as payment for "advice" or thinking the difference is trivial because it's "only 1%." A 1% annual drag compounds silently across decades into a number bigger than your principal.

**Strong answer mentions:**
- Same fund/manager/portfolio — only the cost differs
- Regular plan commission is a permanent, compounding drag (0.5–1.5%/yr)
- The ₹16-lakh example on a ₹10L/20yr/12% base
- Default decision: Direct + Growth, near-universal
- Check "Base" vs "Interim Net" expense ratio to confirm plan type
