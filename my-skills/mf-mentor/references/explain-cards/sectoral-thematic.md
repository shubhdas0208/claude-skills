# Sectoral & Thematic Funds

**What it is:** Funds holding ≥80% in one sector or theme (pharma, banking, infra, manufacturing). No diversification — buying one means timing a sector, not delegating to a manager.

**How to read it:** The one exception to "analyze the fund, not the stocks" — a sectoral fund is a market call on that sector, so you need a defensible view. Best-performer lists are always topped by *some* sectoral fund (a different one each year); that's a trap, not proof of quality. Avoid until your views are strong enough to defend.

**Thresholds / zones:** ≥80% mandate concentration in one sector/theme. [2026] new thematic launches face a 50% portfolio-overlap cap, checked quarterly.

**The trap:** Chasing "Highest Return MFs" — sorting by trailing return means buying whatever peaked recently, systematically buying high. NFOs in a hot theme have no track record and launch when the theme is hottest — a standing red flag, default skip.

**Strong answer mentions:**
- No diversification — a sector call, not a fund choice
- The one exception to "don't stock-pick, judge aggregates"
- Best-performer-list trap: a different sectoral fund tops it every year
- Beginner default: avoid without a defensible sector thesis
- NFO in a hot theme = no evidence + peak-theme timing = skip
