# Multi-Cap vs Flexi-Cap

**What it is:** The deepest structural difference hidden behind similar names. Multi-Cap: **rules decide** the mix — ≥75% equity with **≥25% each** in large, mid, and small-cap, always. Flexi-Cap: **the manager decides** — ≥65% equity, any cap mix, full discretion.

**How to read it:** Multi-cap guarantees small/mid exposure — and guarantees small/mid pain in crashes, since the rule doesn't relax when small-caps get expensive. Flexi-cap is a bet on the manager's allocation judgment; the manager can go 100% large-cap if scared, or load up on small-caps if confident.

**Thresholds / zones:** Multi-Cap ≥25% each bucket, ≥75% total equity. Flexi-Cap ≥65% equity, no cap-mix constraint.

**The trap:** Assuming two flexi-cap funds are similar because they share a label. Check the actual cap split before buying — Parag Parikh Flexi Cap holds foreign stocks and large cash buffers, exactly why its beta sits far below 1; another flexi-cap might run near-100% Indian large-cap.

**Strong answer mentions:**
- Multi-cap = rules-mandated allocation; Flexi-cap = manager discretion
- Multi-cap forces small/mid exposure even when those segments are expensive
- Flexi-cap is fundamentally a bet on the manager's allocation skill
- Must check actual portfolio cap split, not just the category label
- Parag Parikh example: foreign stocks + cash buffer → damped beta (0.6)
