# The Three Tax Buckets (FY 2026-27)

**What it is:** Tax treatment is set by what the fund *holds*. **Bucket 1 — Equity-oriented** (≥65% Indian equity: equity funds, aggressive hybrids, arbitrage, most BAFs). **Bucket 2 — Specified debt-oriented** (>65% debt, bought after Apr 1 2023). **Bucket 3 — The middle** (35–65% equity; since Apr 2025 also gold/international/FoF outside Bucket 2).

**How to read it:** Bucket 1: STCG 20% (≤12mo), LTCG 12.5% (>12mo), **first ₹1.25L LTCG/FY tax-free**, aggregated across equity funds + stocks; plus 0.001% STT. Bucket 2: **all gains at slab rate**, any holding period — no LTCG, no indexation. Bucket 3: ≤24mo slab, >24mo LTCG 12.5% (no exemption — equity-only).

**Thresholds / zones:** 12-month line (Bucket 1) vs 24-month line (Bucket 3); ₹1.25L exemption applies only to Bucket 1.

**The trap:** ELSS "save ₹46,800!" marketing assumes old regime, top slab. Under the new regime (default since AY 2024-25, ~72% of filers) ELSS gives **zero** 80C benefit.

**Strong answer mentions:**
- Bucket set by the fund's actual equity %, not its name
- ₹1.25L LTCG exemption/FY, aggregated across equity funds + stocks
- Bucket 2: no LTCG or indexation — pure slab taxation
- Tax harvesting: redeem up to ₹1.25L LTCG yearly, rebuy, reset cost basis
- ELSS 80C benefit real only under old regime; zero under new
