# Overlap With Your Other Funds

**What it is:** How much a candidate fund's holdings duplicate what you already own, checked pairwise before adding a new fund.

**How to read it:** Two large-cap funds routinely share 60–80% of holdings — buying both means buying the same portfolio twice and calling it diversification. Real diversification means different return engines: different market-cap tiers, styles (growth vs value), geographies, or asset classes — not different AMC logos wrapped around the same stocks. Check overlap before adding any new fund, using a tool like Advisorkhoj's Portfolio Overlap, or by eyeballing each fund's top-15 holdings.

**Thresholds / zones:** Overlap < ~33% is a decent bar for a genuinely additive fund. Two large-cap funds routinely overlap 60–80% — the "fake diversification" example.

**The trap:** Assuming multiple funds automatically means diversification — if two funds share most top holdings, the second one adds cost and complexity without adding real diversification.
