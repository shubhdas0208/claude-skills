# Since-Launch Return

**What it is:** The trailing return over the whole life of the fund, annualized (CAGR per §3.1). The handbook describes what it measures as "whole life of the fund."

**How to read it:** Legitimate use is long-run compounding evidence. The handbook's example — PPFAS at 18.19%/yr since 2013 — is glossed as "that's ₹1 → ~₹9," showing how a since-launch CAGR translates into an actual multiple. Illegitimate use is comparing funds launched in different years/market conditions, since different launch dates make the comparison apples-to-oranges — a fund launched into a bull market and one launched into a crash show very different since-launch numbers for reasons unrelated to skill.

**Thresholds / zones:** None given.

**The trap:** Ranking funds against each other by since-launch return without checking their launch dates — the number is real evidence about that one fund's history, but it is not a fair cross-fund comparison unless launch timing lines up.

**Strong answer mentions:**
- Since-launch is CAGR over the fund's entire life
- Legitimate use: seeing the long-run compounding evidence for that one fund
- Illegitimate use: cross-fund comparison when launch dates/market conditions differ
- The PPFAS example (18.19%/yr since 2013 ≈ ₹1 → ~₹9) shows how to translate a CAGR into a real multiple
- Different launch dates make since-launch numbers apples-to-oranges across funds
