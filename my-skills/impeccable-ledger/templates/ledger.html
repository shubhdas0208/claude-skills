What happens next

  1. Decide. Approve, reject, or ask for changes, right under each comparison. A note or a screenshot on any block counts as a change request.

  4. Next round. Touched files are backed up, then approved rows are built. Change requests come back revised at the top. Rejected rows never return. The whole UI is reviewed again, changed screens first.
